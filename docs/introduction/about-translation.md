# About this translation

Each verse is studied in four parts, in this order:

1. **Sanskrit verse** in Devanagari, with its reference (Prakaraṇa.Sarga.Śloka).
2. **Word by word** — each Sanskrit word in Devanagari, arranged in prose order (anvaya), with its meaning in brackets.
3. **Translation** — a plain English rendering of the whole verse.
4. **Pointers for Reflection** — parallels, key words and cross-references to the Gītā and Upaniṣads, from which my personal reflection grows.

The Sanskrit text follows the Nirṇaya Sāgar edition. All word meanings and translations here are my own.

Key terms are rendered the same way every time; see the [Glossary](../glossary.md).

## Linking to a verse

Every verse has its own address. For example, verse 1.1.2 is at
`…/1-vairagya/sarga-001/#1-1-2`. Share these links freely.
