# Why I love Yoga Vasistha

1. **The Lord as student.** In the Bhagavad Gītā the Lord is the Guru, teaching Arjuna. In the Yoga Vasiṣṭha the Lord is the student: श्रीराम sits before Vasiṣṭha to learn. Together the two texts show the teaching from both sides.

2. **Questions worth asking.** Often we do not even know what to ask. Here श्रीराम, still a teenager, asks the questions that matter most — about suffering, time, the mind and the meaning of life. The text teaches us how to ask before it teaches us the answers.

3. **A synthesis.** The text is Advaita Vedānta at its core, yet it weaves in insights from other traditions and philosophies, such as Jainism, Buddhism and Kashmir Śaivism, into one beautiful whole.

4. **Wisdom lived, not renounced.** श्रीराम does not take up renunciation. Having awakened, he goes on to live a full householder life, showing that liberation is possible in the midst of duty and the world.
