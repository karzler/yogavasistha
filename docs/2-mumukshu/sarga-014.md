# Sarga 14

*Mumukṣu Prakaraṇa · The second gatekeeper: inquiry (54 verses)*

!!! info "Sarga 14 at a glance"
    The second gatekeeper: inquiry (विचार).

    1. Inquiry, made constantly with a pure mind, is the great medicine for saṃsāra; it cuts the forest of calamity at the root (2.14.1–2.14.10).
    2. Minds that inquire float in calamity like gourds; thoughtlessness is a drunken sleep in which evils appear like ghosts in the dark (2.14.11–2.14.27).
    3. The fruit of inquiry is oneness and desirelessness; the mind becomes like the sky, a witness that lets go of the past and meets the present (2.14.28–2.14.36).
    4. "Who am I? Whose is saṃsāra?" — ask this even in calamity. Inquiry is a divine eye; never give it up (2.14.37–2.14.49).
    5. Definition and fruit: inquiry → truth → rest in the Self → peace → the end of all sorrow (2.14.50–2.14.54).

## Verse 2.14.1 { #2-14-1 }

<p class="shloka">
<span class="speaker">श्रीवसिष्ठ उवाच ।</span><br>
शास्त्रावबोधामलया धिया परमपूतया ।<br>
कर्तव्यः कारणज्ञेन विचारोऽनिशमात्मनः ॥ २.१४.१ ॥
</p>

<p class="anvaya">
कारण-ज्ञेन (by one who knows causes) शास्त्र-अवबोध-अमलया परम-पूतया धिया (with a mind made spotless and wholly pure by understanding scripture) आत्मनः विचारः (inquiry into oneself) अनिशं कर्तव्यः (should be made constantly).
</p>

**Translation:** Śrī Vasiṣṭha said: "One who understands causes should inquire into the Self constantly, with a mind made spotless and pure by understanding scripture."

**Pointers for Reflection:**

- **The second gatekeeper: विचार.** Inquiry — reflective, reasoned investigation into one's own nature. Sarga 14 is the Yoga Vasiṣṭha's great hymn to it.
- **Constantly.** Not an occasional exercise but a way of living. Ramaṇa Maharṣi, in the twentieth century, made this verse's teaching — self-inquiry, *ātma-vicāra* — the heart of his instruction, and often quoted the Yoga Vasiṣṭha.

## Verse 2.14.2 { #2-14-2 }

<p class="shloka">
विचारात्तीक्ष्णतामेत्य धीः पश्यति परं पदम् ।<br>
दीर्घसंसाररोगस्य विचारो हि महौषधम् ॥ २.१४.२ ॥
</p>

<p class="anvaya">
विचारात् (through inquiry) तीक्ष्णताम् एत्य (growing sharp) धीः (the mind) परं पदं पश्यति (sees the supreme state); विचारः हि (inquiry indeed) दीर्घ-संसार-रोगस्य महा-औषधम् (is the great medicine for the long disease of saṃsāra).
</p>

**Translation:** "Sharpened by inquiry, the mind sees the supreme state. Inquiry is the great medicine for the long disease of saṃsāra."

**Pointers for Reflection:**

- **Sharpening the mind.** The Kaṭha: <span class="parallel">दृश्यते त्वग्र्यया बुद्ध्या सूक्ष्मया सूक्ष्मदर्शिभिः</span> — *it is seen by those of subtle vision, with a sharp and subtle intellect* (1.3.12).

## Verse 2.14.3 { #2-14-3 }

<p class="shloka">
आपद्वनमनन्तेहापरिपल्लविताकृति ।<br>
विचारक्रकचच्छिन्नं नैव भूयः प्ररोहति ॥ २.१४.३ ॥
</p>

<p class="anvaya">
अनन्त-ईहा-परिपल्लवित-आकृति (whose form is covered with the endless shoots of desire) आपत्-वनम् (the forest of calamities) विचार-क्रकच-छिन्नं (cut down by the saw of inquiry) भूयः न एव प्ररोहति (never grows again).
</p>

**Translation:** "The forest of calamities, thick with the endless shoots of desire, once cut down by the saw of inquiry, never grows again."

**Pointers for Reflection:**

- **Cut at the root.** Other remedies trim branches; inquiry cuts the root. The Gītā: <span class="parallel">तस्मादज्ञानसंभूतं हृत्स्थं ज्ञानासिनात्मनः । छित्त्वैनं संशयं</span> — *cutting with the sword of knowledge this doubt born of ignorance in the heart* (4.42).

## Verse 2.14.4 { #2-14-4 }

<p class="shloka">
मोहेन बन्धुनाशेषु संकटेषु शमेषु च ।<br>
सर्वं व्याप्तं महाप्राज्ञ विचारो हि सतां गतिः ॥ २.१४.४ ॥
</p>

<p class="anvaya">
महा-प्राज्ञ (O very wise one), बन्धु-नाशेषु संकटेषु शमेषु च (in loss of kin, in crises, and in quiet times) सर्वं मोहेन व्याप्तम् (everything is pervaded by delusion); सतां गतिः विचारः हि (inquiry is the refuge of the good).
</p>

**Translation:** "Wise one, in the loss of loved ones, in crises and even in quiet times, delusion pervades everything; inquiry is the refuge of the good."

**Pointers for Reflection:**

- **In every condition.** Not only in hardship: delusion is present in good times too. Inquiry is needed always.

## Verse 2.14.5 { #2-14-5 }

<p class="shloka">
न विचारं विना कश्चिदुपायोऽस्ति विपश्चिताम् ।<br>
विचारादशुभं त्यक्त्वा शुभमायाति धीः सताम् ॥ २.१४.५ ॥
</p>

<p class="anvaya">
विपश्चितां (for the wise) विचारं विना (without inquiry) कश्चित् उपायः न अस्ति (there is no means); विचारात् (through inquiry) सतां धीः (the mind of the good) अशुभं त्यक्त्वा शुभम् आयाति (leaves the harmful and comes to the good).
</p>

**Translation:** "For the wise there is no means other than inquiry. Through it, the mind of the good leaves the harmful and reaches the good."

**Pointers for Reflection:**

- **Inquiry redirects.** It is the tool for the river-turning of 2.9.30.

## Verse 2.14.6 { #2-14-6 }

<p class="shloka">
बलं बुद्धिश्च तेजश्च प्रतिपत्तिः क्रियाफलम् ।<br>
फलन्त्येतानि सर्वाणि विचारेणैव धीमताम् ॥ २.१४.६ ॥
</p>

<p class="anvaya">
बलं बुद्धिः च तेजः च (strength, intelligence, vigour) प्रतिपत्तिः (right understanding) क्रिया-फलम् (the fruit of action) — एतानि सर्वाणि (all these) धीमतां विचारेण एव फलन्ति (bear fruit for the wise through inquiry alone).
</p>

**Translation:** "Strength, intelligence, vigour, right understanding, the success of action — all these bear fruit for the wise through inquiry alone."

**Pointers for Reflection:**

- **Inquiry in worldly life too.** Not only for mokṣa: every undertaking succeeds better with reflection.

## Verse 2.14.7 { #2-14-7 }

<p class="shloka">
युक्तायुक्तमहादीपमभिवाञ्छितसाधकम् ।<br>
स्फारं विचारमाश्रित्य संसारजलधिं तरेत् ॥ २.१४.७ ॥
</p>

<p class="anvaya">
युक्त-अयुक्त-महा-दीपम् (the great lamp that shows what is fitting and unfitting) अभिवाञ्छित-साधकम् (which achieves what is desired) स्फारं विचारम् आश्रित्य (relying on vast inquiry) संसार-जलधिं तरेत् (one should cross the ocean of saṃsāra).
</p>

**Translation:** "Relying on broad inquiry — the great lamp that shows what is fitting and what is not, and achieves what one seeks — cross the ocean of saṃsāra."

**Pointers for Reflection:**

- **A lamp for choices.** Inquiry shows right from wrong in each situation.

## Verse 2.14.8 { #2-14-8 }

<p class="shloka">
आलूनहृदयाम्भोजान्महामोहमतङ्गजान् ।<br>
विदारयति शुद्धात्मा विचारो नाम केसरी ॥ २.१४.८ ॥
</p>

<p class="anvaya">
विचारः नाम केसरी (the lion called inquiry) शुद्ध-आत्मा (pure in nature) आलून-हृदय-अम्भोजान् (who tear up the lotus of the heart) महा-मोह-मतङ्ग-जान् (the great elephants of delusion) विदारयति (tears apart).
</p>

**Translation:** "Inquiry is a pure lion that tears apart the great elephants of delusion, which trample the lotus of the heart."

**Pointers for Reflection:**

- **Lion against elephants.** Delusion is huge and heavy; inquiry is smaller but fiercer.

## Verse 2.14.9 { #2-14-9 }

<p class="shloka">
मूढाः कालवशेनेह यद्गताः परमं पदम् ।<br>
तद्विचारप्रदीपस्य विजृम्भितमनुत्तमम् ॥ २.१४.९ ॥
</p>

<p class="anvaya">
यत् (that) इह (here) मूढाः (even the deluded) काल-वशेन (in time) परमं पदं गताः (have reached the supreme state) — तत् (that) विचार-प्रदीपस्य अनुत्तमं विजृम्भितम् (is the unsurpassed work of the lamp of inquiry).
</p>

**Translation:** "That even the deluded have, in time, reached the supreme state is the unsurpassed work of the lamp of inquiry."

**Pointers for Reflection:**

- **Hope for everyone.** No one is too dull for inquiry. In time, its light reaches all.

## Verse 2.14.10 { #2-14-10 }

<p class="shloka">
राज्यानि संपदः स्फारा भोगो मोक्षश्च शाश्वतः ।<br>
विचारकल्पवृक्षस्य फलान्येतानि राघव ॥ २.१४.१० ॥
</p>

<p class="anvaya">
राघव (O Rāghava), राज्यानि (kingdoms) स्फाराः संपदः (great riches) भोगः (enjoyment) शाश्वतः मोक्षः च (and lasting liberation) — एतानि (these) विचार-कल्प-वृक्षस्य फलानि (are the fruits of the wishing tree of inquiry).
</p>

**Translation:** "Kingdoms, great riches, enjoyment and lasting liberation — Rāghava, these are the fruits of the wishing tree of inquiry."

**Pointers for Reflection:**

- **A wishing tree.** Inquiry yields whatever one rightly seeks.

## Verse 2.14.11 { #2-14-11 }

<p class="shloka">
या विवेकविकासिन्यो मतयो महतामिह ।<br>
न ता विपदि मज्जन्ति तुम्बकानीव वारिणि ॥ २.१४.११ ॥
</p>

<p class="anvaya">
इह (here) महतां (of the great) याः विवेक-विकासिन्यः मतयः (minds that blossom with discernment) ताः (they) विपदि न मज्जन्ति (do not sink in calamity), वारिणि तुम्बकानि इव (as gourds [do not sink] in water).
</p>

**Translation:** "The minds of the great, blossoming with discernment, do not sink in calamity, as dried gourds do not sink in water."

**Pointers for Reflection:**

- **The floating gourd.** Hollow gourds were used as floats to cross rivers. A mind emptied of delusion floats.

## Verse 2.14.12 { #2-14-12 }

<p class="shloka">
विचारोदयकारिण्या धिया व्यवहरन्ति ये ।<br>
फलानामत्युदाराणां भाजनं हि भवन्ति ते ॥ २.१४.१२ ॥
</p>

<p class="anvaya">
ये (those who) विचार-उदय-कारिण्या धिया (with a mind that makes inquiry arise) व्यवहरन्ति (conduct their affairs), ते हि (they indeed) अति-उदाराणां फलानां भाजनं भवन्ति (become vessels of the noblest fruits).
</p>

**Translation:** "Those who conduct their affairs with a mind that keeps inquiry alive become vessels of the noblest fruits."

**Pointers for Reflection:**

- **Inquiry in daily affairs.** Not only in meditation but in business, family and governance.

## Verse 2.14.13 { #2-14-13 }

<p class="shloka">
मूर्खहृत्काननस्थानामाशाप्रथमरोधिनाम् ।<br>
अविचारकरञ्जानां मञ्जर्यो दुःखरीतयः ॥ २.१४.१३ ॥
</p>

<p class="anvaya">
मूर्ख-हृत्-कानन-स्थानाम् (growing in the forest of the fool's heart) आशा-प्रथम-रोधिनां (first blocking hope) अविचार-करञ्जानां (of the karañja trees of thoughtlessness) मञ्जर्यः (the blossoms) दुःख-रीतयः (are the ways of sorrow).
</p>

**Translation:** "In the forest of the fool's heart grow the karañja trees of thoughtlessness, which block every hope; their blossoms are the many forms of sorrow."

**Pointers for Reflection:**

- **Bitter trees.** The karañja is known for its bitter fruit. Thoughtlessness bears sorrow as naturally.

## Verse 2.14.14 { #2-14-14 }

<p class="shloka">
कज्जलक्षोदमलिना मदिरामदधर्मिणी ।<br>
अविचारमयी निद्रा यातु ते राघव क्षयम् ॥ २.१४.१४ ॥
</p>

<p class="anvaya">
राघव (O Rāghava), कज्जल-क्षोद-मलिना (dark as soot) मदिरा-मद-धर्मिणी (with the nature of drunkenness) अविचार-मयी निद्रा (the sleep of thoughtlessness) ते क्षयं यातु (may it end for you).
</p>

**Translation:** "Rāghava, may the sleep of thoughtlessness — black as soot, like a drunkard's stupor — come to an end for you."

**Pointers for Reflection:**

- **A blessing.** Vasiṣṭha prays for Rāma's waking.

## Verse 2.14.15 { #2-14-15 }

<p class="shloka">
महापदतिदीर्घेषु सद्विचारपरो नरः ।<br>
न निमज्जति मोहेषु तेजोराशिस्तमःस्विव ॥ २.१४.१५ ॥
</p>

<p class="anvaya">
सत्-विचार-परः नरः (a person devoted to good inquiry) महा-आपत्-अति-दीर्घेषु मोहेषु (in great, long-lasting calamities and delusions) न निमज्जति (does not sink), तेजो-राशिः तमःसु इव (as a mass of light [does not sink] in darkness).
</p>

**Translation:** "One devoted to true inquiry does not sink in great and long calamities and delusions, as a mass of light does not sink in darkness."

**Pointers for Reflection:**

- **Light cannot drown in darkness.** Light and dark are not two equal forces; light simply dispels.

## Verse 2.14.16 { #2-14-16 }

<p class="shloka">
मानसे सरसि स्वच्छे विचारकमलोत्करः ।<br>
नूनं विकसितो यस्य हिमवानिव भाति सः ॥ २.१४.१६ ॥
</p>

<p class="anvaya">
यस्य (whose) स्वच्छे मानसे सरसि (in the clear lake of the mind) विचार-कमल-उत्करः (a cluster of lotuses of inquiry) नूनं विकसितः (has surely bloomed), सः हिमवान् इव भाति (he shines like the Himālaya).
</p>

**Translation:** "One in the clear lake of whose mind the lotuses of inquiry have blossomed shines like the Himālaya."

**Pointers for Reflection:**

- **Mānasa lake.** A pun: मानस is both "of the mind" and the sacred lake Mānasarovara at the foot of Kailāsa in the Himālaya.

## Verse 2.14.17 { #2-14-17 }

<p class="shloka">
विचारविकला यस्य मतिर्मान्द्यमुपेयुषः ।<br>
तस्योदेत्यशनिश्चन्द्रान्मुधा यक्षः शिशोरिव ॥ २.१४.१७ ॥
</p>

<p class="anvaya">
यस्य मतिः विचार-विकला (whose mind lacks inquiry) मान्द्यम् उपेयुषः (and has grown dull), तस्य (for him) चन्द्रात् अशनिः उदेति (a thunderbolt rises from the moon), शिशोः मुधा यक्षः इव (as a phantom for a child).
</p>

**Translation:** "For one whose mind lacks inquiry and has grown dull, even the moon seems to throw thunderbolts, as a child sees a phantom where there is none."

**Pointers for Reflection:**

- **Fear from the harmless.** Without inquiry, even the gentle becomes threatening.

## Verse 2.14.18 { #2-14-18 }

<p class="shloka">
दुःखखण्डकमस्थूलं विपन्नवलतामधुः ।<br>
राम दूरे परित्याज्यो निर्विवेको नराधमः ॥ २.१४.१८ ॥

<p class="anvaya">

## Verse 2.14.19 { #2-14-19 }

<p class="shloka">
ये केचन दुरारम्भा दुराचारा दुराधयः ।<br>
अविचारेण ते भान्ति वेतालास्तमसा यथा ॥ २.१४.१९ ॥
</p>

<p class="anvaya">

## Verse 2.14.21 { #2-14-21 }

## Verse 2.14.22 { #2-14-22 }

<p class="shloka">
विवेकितोदिता देहे सर्वं शीतलयत्यलम् ।<br>
अलंकरोति चात्यन्तं ज्योत्स्नेव भुवनं यथा ॥ २.१४.२२ ॥

## Verse 2.14.44 { #2-14-44 }

<p class="shloka">
विचारकान्तमतयो नानेकेषु पुनःपुनः ।<br>
लुठन्ति दुःखश्वभ्रेषु ज्ञाताध्वगतयो नराः ॥ २.१४.४४ ॥
</p>

<p class="anvaya">
विचार-कान्त-मतयः (whose minds are lovely with inquiry) ज्ञात-अध्व-गतयः नराः (people who know the path) अनेकेषु दुःख-श्वभ्रेषु (in the many pits of sorrow) पुनः पुनः न लुठन्ति (do not roll again and again).
</p>

**Translation:** "People whose minds are made lovely by inquiry, who know the way, do not roll again and again into the many pits of sorrow."

**Pointers for Reflection:**

- **Knowing the road.** One who knows the path does not keep falling into the same ditches.

## Verse 2.14.45 { #2-14-45 }

<p class="shloka">
नच रौति तथा रोगी नानर्थशतजर्जरः ।<br>
अविचारविनष्टात्मा यथाऽज्ञः परिरोदिति ॥ २.१४.४५ ॥
</p>

<p class="anvaya">
यथा (as) अविचार-विनष्ट-आत्मा अज्ञः परिरोदिति (the ignorant one, ruined by thoughtlessness, wails) तथा (so much) न च रोगी रौति (does not even the sick one cry) न अनर्थ-शत-जर्जरः (nor one shattered by a hundred misfortunes).
</p>

**Translation:** "Neither a sick man nor one broken by a hundred misfortunes cries as bitterly as the ignorant one ruined by thoughtlessness."

**Pointers for Reflection:**

- **Self-made suffering is the worst.** It has no outer cause to blame and no outer cure.

## Verse 2.14.46 { #2-14-46 }

<p class="shloka">
वरं कर्दममेकत्वं मलकीटकता वरम् ।<br>
वरमन्धगुहाहित्वं न नरस्याविचारिता ॥ २.१४.४६ ॥
</p>

<p class="anvaya">
कर्दमे एकत्वं वरं (better to be one with the mud) मल-कीटकता वरम् (better to be a worm in filth) अन्ध-गुहा-अहित्वं वरम् (better to be a snake in a dark cave) — न नरस्य अविचारिता (than for a human to be thoughtless).
</p>

**Translation:** "Better to be one with the mud, better to be a worm in filth, better to be a snake in a dark cave, than for a human being to live without inquiry."

**Pointers for Reflection:**

- **Socrates' echo.** In another tradition: *the unexamined life is not worth living* (Plato, Apology 38a). Vasiṣṭha puts it even more sharply.

## Verse 2.14.47 { #2-14-47 }

<p class="shloka">
सर्वानर्थनिजावासं सर्वसाधुतिरस्कृतम् ।<br>
सर्वदौस्थित्यसीमान्तमविचारं परित्यजेत् ॥ २.१४.४७ ॥
</p>

<p class="anvaya">
सर्व-अनर्थ-निज-आवासं (the very home of all evils) सर्व-साधु-तिरस्कृतम् (scorned by all the good) सर्व-दौस्थित्य-सीमान्तम् (the extreme of all misfortune) अविचारं परित्यजेत् (one should give up thoughtlessness).
</p>

**Translation:** "Give up thoughtlessness: it is the very home of all evils, scorned by all the good, the utmost of every misfortune."

**Pointers for Reflection:**

- **The root fault named.** अविचार — not-inquiring — is the soil of all other faults.

## Verse 2.14.48 { #2-14-48 }

<p class="shloka">
नित्यं विचारयुक्तेन भवितव्यं महात्मना ।<br>
तथान्धकूपे पततां विचारो ह्यवलम्बनम् ॥ २.१४.४८ ॥
</p>

<p class="anvaya">
महा-आत्मना (by a great soul) नित्यं विचार-युक्तेन भवितव्यम् (one should always be joined with inquiry); तथा (for) अन्ध-कूपे पतताम् (for those falling into a dark well) विचारः हि अवलम्बनम् (inquiry is the support).
</p>

**Translation:** "A great soul should always be joined with inquiry; for those falling into the dark well, inquiry is the one thing to hold on to."

**Pointers for Reflection:**

- **A rope in the well.** When everything else gives way, inquiry holds.

## Verse 2.14.49 { #2-14-49 }

<p class="shloka">
स्वयमेवात्मनात्मानमवष्टभ्य विचारतः ।<br>
संसारमोहजलधेस्तारयेत्स्वमनोमृगम् ॥ २.१४.४९ ॥
</p>

<p class="anvaya">
स्वयम् एव (by oneself) आत्मना आत्मानं विचारतः अवष्टभ्य (steadying oneself by oneself through inquiry) संसार-मोह-जलधेः (across the ocean of saṃsāra's delusion) स्व-मनो-मृगं तारयेत् (one should carry the deer of one's mind).
</p>

**Translation:** "By oneself, steadying the Self by the Self through inquiry, one should carry the deer of one's mind across the ocean of saṃsāra's delusion."

**Pointers for Reflection:**

- **Self by Self.** The Gītā: <span class="parallel">उद्धरेदात्मनात्मानम्</span> (6.5). No one else can do the inquiry for us.

## Verse 2.14.50 { #2-14-50 }

<p class="shloka">
कोऽहं कथमयं दोषः संसाराख्य उपागतः ।<br>
न्यायेनेति परामर्शो विचार इति कथ्यते ॥ २.१४.५० ॥
</p>

<p class="anvaya">
"अहं कः (who am I)? अयं संसार-आख्यः दोषः कथम् उपागतः (how did this fault called saṃsāra come about)?" इति न्यायेन परामर्शः (such reasoned reflection) विचारः इति कथ्यते (is called inquiry).
</p>

**Translation:** "'Who am I? How did this fault called saṃsāra come about?' — such reasoned reflection is called inquiry."

**Pointers for Reflection:**

- **A definition of विचार.** Two questions, examined with reason (न्याय): the nature of the self, and the origin of bondage. The Aparokṣānubhūti (attributed to Śaṅkara) gives almost the same definition: <span class="parallel">कोऽहं कथमिदं जातं को वै कर्तास्य विद्यते । उपादानं किमस्तीह विचारः सोऽयमीदृशः</span> — *who am I? how did this arise? who is its maker, and what its material? Such is inquiry* (12)

## Verse 2.14.51 { #2-14-51 }

<p class="shloka">
अन्धान्धमोहसुधनं चिरं दुःखाय केवलम् ।<br>
कृतं शिलाया हृदयं दुर्मतेश्चाविचारिणः ॥ २.१४.५१ ॥
</p>

<p class="anvaya">
दुर्मतेः अविचारिणः च (of the foolish and thoughtless) हृदयं (the heart) शिलायाः कृतम् (as if made of stone) अन्ध-अन्ध-मोह-सुधनम् (thick with blind delusion) केवलं चिरं दुःखाय (serves only long sorrow).
</p>

**Translation:** "The heart of the foolish and thoughtless is like stone, thick with blind delusion; it serves only for long sorrow."

**Pointers for Reflection:**

- **Stone heart.** Hard, cold, impervious. Inquiry softens it.

## Verse 2.14.52 { #2-14-52 }

<p class="shloka">
भावाभावग्रहोत्सर्गदृशामिह हि राघव ।<br>
न विचारादृते तत्त्वं ज्ञायते साधु किंचन ॥ २.१४.५२ ॥
</p>

<p class="anvaya">
राघव (O Rāghava), इह हि (here indeed) भाव-अभाव-ग्रह-उत्सर्ग-दृशां (for those whose view is grasping at being and rejecting non-being) विचारात् ऋते (without inquiry) किंचन तत्त्वं साधु न ज्ञायते (no truth at all is rightly known).
</p>

**Translation:** "Rāghava, for those whose view is caught up in grasping 'it is' and rejecting 'it is not', no truth is rightly known without inquiry."

**Pointers for Reflection:**

- **Beyond 'is' and 'is not'.** Nāgārjuna too sought a middle way between existence and non-existence. Jaina *anekāntavāda* teaches that reality cannot be captured by a single one-sided assertion.

## Verse 2.14.53 { #2-14-53 }

<p class="shloka">
विचाराज्ज्ञायते तत्त्वं तत्त्वाद्विश्रान्तिरात्मनि ।<br>
अतो मनसि शान्तत्वं सर्वदुःखपरिक्षयः ॥ २.१४.५३ ॥
</p>

<p class="anvaya">
विचारात् तत्त्वं ज्ञायते (by inquiry the truth is known); तत्त्वात् आत्मनि विश्रान्तिः (from the truth, rest in the Self); अतः मनसि शान्तत्वं (from that, peace in the mind) सर्व-दुःख-परिक्षयः (and the end of all sorrow).
</p>

**Translation:** "Through inquiry the truth is known; from the truth comes rest in the Self; from that, peace of mind and the end of all sorrow."

**Pointers for Reflection:**

- **The chain of liberation.** Inquiry → truth → rest in the Self → peace → end of sorrow. This one verse maps the whole path of the Yoga Vasiṣṭha.
- **Rest (विश्रान्ति)** — what Śuka found (2.1.42) and what Rāma seeks (2.1.4).

## Verse 2.14.54 { #2-14-54 }

<p class="shloka">
सफलता फलते भुवि कर्मणां प्रकटतां किल गच्छति उत्तमाम् ।<br>
स्फुटविचारदृशैव विचारिता शमवते भवते च विरोचताम् ॥ २.१४.५४ ॥
</p>

<p class="anvaya">
भुवि (on earth) कर्मणां सफलता (the success of actions) फलते (bears fruit) उत्तमां प्रकटतां किल गच्छति (and comes to full display); स्फुट-विचार-दृशा एव विचारिता (examined with the clear eye of inquiry) [सा] शमवते भवते च विरोचताम् (may it shine for you, who have calm).
</p>

**Translation:** "The success of actions on earth bears fruit and shows itself fully when examined with the clear eye of inquiry. May it shine for you, who have calm."

**Pointers for Reflection:**

- **Blessing for Rāma.** Inquiry brings success in action as well as freedom.
