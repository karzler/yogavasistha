# 3. Utpatti Prakaraṇa

<p class="shloka">उत्पत्तिप्रकरणम्</p>

**Origination.** Having prepared the seeker, Vasiṣṭha now turns to knowledge itself. This book asks how the world arises — and answers that it arises in consciousness, as a dream arises in the dreamer. Bondage is taking the seen as real; freedom is the end of that. The teaching is carried by some of the text's best-known stories: Līlā, who travels through worlds within worlds; the demoness Karkaṭī; the sons of Indu; Ahalyā; and others.
